## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-24

### Added

- First version: the sites of a Plausible Analytics account (plausible.io, or a
  self-hosted Community Edition 2.1 or later, with a Stats API key) as Gladys
  devices, with the visitors right now and the visitors, visits, page views,
  bounce rate, visit duration and most viewed page of the day.
- Scene triggers: visitor spike, first visitor of the day, no visit for a
  while.
- Scene action "Read the figures of a site", with a ready-made sentence.
- Dashboard widget "Plausible today".
- "Test the connection" action, texts in English, French, German and Spanish.
